# Backlink strategy

The goal is a small number of **relevant, trustworthy** links that fit a neutral, ethics-bound ADR practice. Quality over volume.

## Ground rules
- **No paid links, link farms, private blog networks, or link swaps.** They risk Google penalties and don't fit the practice's ethics posture.
- **Ethics screen first.** Before listing anywhere, confirm the site isn't aimed at consumer-business disputes or connected to the Consumer Protection Division or the Office of the Attorney General. Check against Maryland Public Ethics Law §5-502 and the "What we do not handle" lists.
- **Consistent NAP** (name, phone, email, URL) everywhere, matching the website and schema exactly:
  - Transformative Life Solutions · +1 (240) 650-0007 · LifeSolutions@LetTLSHelp.com · https://lettlshelp.com/life-solutions/
  - Transformative Leadership Systems · +1 (240) 650-0007 · LeadershipSystems@LetTLSHelp.com · https://lettlshelp.com/leadership-systems/
  - If the vanity domains launch later, update every citation as part of that migration so listings
    don't point at redirects indefinitely.
- **Only list real credentials.** Rosters and memberships go on the site only after they're confirmed (see the Credentials slot).
- **Link to the right site.** Family and interpersonal links go to Life Solutions; B2B, arbitration, and negotiation links go to Leadership Systems.
- **Verify before acting.** Organizations below are suggestions; confirm each one's current eligibility rules, fees, and listing terms first.

## Phase 1: Foundations (launch month)
| Action | Site | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Google Business Profile (service-area business if there's no public office) | Both, separately | Map/local results, reviews, a trusted link |
| Bing Places; Apple Business Connect | Both | Same NAP on the other major map platforms |
| LinkedIn company page for each practice, linked from Tanika's profile (linkedin.com/in/lettlshelp) | Both | Entity signal; matches `sameAs` in schema |
| Link the two sister sites to each other (footer + cross-link bands, already built) | Both | Helps users and crawlers; low SEO weight on its own |
| Search Console + sitemap submission | Both | Discovery; shows the Links report over time |

## Phase 2: Professional & ADR ecosystem (months 1–3)
Referenced in the owner's guides or natural fits. Confirm eligibility first.
- **Maryland Program for Mediator Excellence (MPME):** roster/listing, if Tanika is or becomes MPME-rostered.
- **Community Mediation Maryland** and local community mediation centers: volunteer, training, or partner listings where appropriate.
- **Maryland Council for Dispute Resolution** and other state or regional ADR associations: member directories.
- **National ADR associations and mediator directories** (e.g., Mediate.com): professional profile pages.
- **University ADR and law-school dispute resolution programs:** guest lectures, trainings, or resource pages.

## Phase 3: Community & B2B relationships (months 2–6)
**Life Solutions:**
- Family-support nonprofits, faith and community organizations, libraries, and co-parenting education programs.
- Offer workshops or talks (e.g., "Communicating through separation"); host pages often link to the facilitator.

**Leadership Systems:**
- Chambers of commerce and business associations (member directories).
- Nonprofit associations and board-governance networks.
- HR and leadership groups: webinars on workplace conflict and board governance.
- Stay within non-consumer-facing organizations.

## Phase 4: Content that earns links (ongoing)
Publish useful, original resources others want to cite, one or two posts a month per site (see `docs/seo-plan.md`):
- **Life:** "What happens in divorce mediation?", "Building a parenting plan: questions to discuss", and a plain-language guide to eligibility screening.
- **Leadership:** "Mediation vs. arbitration vs. med-arb", "When a neutral helps in NDA or contract negotiations", and a board governance conflict checklist.
- Offer these to partner organizations as resources, and pitch guest articles or podcast interviews on trauma-informed, culturally grounded conflict resolution.
- **Never** publish invented case studies, client stories, or statistics.

## Measure & maintain
- **Monthly:** Search Console **Links** report (top linking sites, most-linked pages) and Performance queries for the target keywords.
- **Quarterly:** audit NAP consistency across listings; fix outdated directory entries.
- Disavow only if a clearly spammy link pattern appears; don't disavow by default.
- Track outreach in a simple sheet: organization, contact, ethics-screen result, date, status, and link URL.
