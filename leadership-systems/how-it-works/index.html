How our process works

Structured, neutral, and transparent from the first conversation.

What to expect

  1. Reach out

    Call or text (240) 650-0007, send an email, or begin intake online.

  2. Eligibility screening

    Written and verbal screening confirms your organization and matter are eligible. Every decision is documented.

  3. Confirm next steps

    If it's a fit, we agree on the right process. If not, we explain why and share free supportive referrals.

  4. Meet virtually or in person

    Sessions for eligible B2B clients, in the setting that works.

Step one

Begin intake online

If we can't assist, we'll point you to support

If we are unable to facilitate your matter, we will explain why and provide free supportive referrals. Suggested referrals may include:

  • Maryland's Consumer Protection Division
  • Maryland Courts Self‑Help Center
  • People's Law Library
  • Community Mediation Maryland
  • MPME‑rostered ADR practitioners
  • Better Business Bureau
  • Crisis Text Line

Before you begin

  • We provide neutral ADR services only. We do not provide legal advice, legal representation, or legal advocacy.
  • We provide conflict‑coaching and organizational facilitation services only. We do not provide medical or mental health counseling or clinical therapeutic services.
