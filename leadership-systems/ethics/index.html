Ethics & Compliance

Our commitment to ethical practice

Neutral, transparent, and structured to avoid conflicts of interest.

Compliance with Maryland Public Ethics Law §5‑502

Transformative Leadership Systems operates in full compliance with Maryland Public Ethics Law §5‑502 and maintains strict boundaries to avoid conflicts of interest.

We do not provide services related to consumer‑business disputes or any matter that falls under Maryland consumer protection laws or the authority of the Office of the Attorney General.

What we focus on

Private, non‑consumer‑facing organizational conflicts, including:

  • Internal workplace disputes

    Conflict within teams, departments, and staff.

  • Leadership & partnership conflicts

    Disagreements among leaders, co-owners, and business partners.

  • Board governance issues

    Governance challenges among board members and leadership.

  • Vendor–supplier disputes

    Disputes between non‑consumer‑facing entities.

What we do not handle

We do not mediate, arbitrate, coach, or negotiate matters involving:

  • Consumer‑business disputes
  • Any business that sells goods or services to consumers, such as auto sales or repairs, home improvement, contractors, retail, hospitality, telecom, or finance
  • Any matter involving a business regulated by or investigated under Maryland consumer protection laws
  • Any case where a party has filed a complaint with, been contacted by, or been investigated by the Maryland Office of the Attorney General
  • Any matter that could reasonably appear connected to the work of the Consumer Protection Division of Maryland

If your matter involves a consumer transaction, or if you are a consumer‑facing company or organization, Transformative Leadership Systems cannot assist due to statutory ethics requirements under Maryland Public Ethics Law §5‑502.

Screening protocol

Transformative Leadership Systems uses written and verbal screening processes to determine client eligibility and maintains documentation of all screening decisions. If we are unable to facilitate your matter, we will explain why and provide free supportive referrals.

Suggested referrals

  • Maryland's Consumer Protection Division
  • Maryland Courts Self‑Help Center
  • People's Law Library
  • Community Mediation Maryland
  • MPME‑rostered ADR practitioners
  • Better Business Bureau
  • Crisis Text Line
