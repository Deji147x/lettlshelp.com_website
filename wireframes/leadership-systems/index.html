FAQ

Common questions

What is the difference between mediation, arbitration, and med-arb?

In mediation, a neutral helps the parties reach their own agreement. In arbitration, a neutral hears each side and issues a decision that may be binding or non-binding, depending on what the parties agree to. Med-arb combines the two: mediation first, then arbitration for any issues that remain.
