Screening questions

Thank you for your willingness to complete our screening tool for Transformative Life Solutions. Once we have your responses, we can fully determine if it is appropriate to proceed and offer services.

We thoroughly screen all potential clients before discussing services in detail. Please answer the following questions and, when appropriate, provide additional details.

Your details

    Please identify the type of service(s) you are seeking.

    • Family & Parenting Mediation: support for separation, parenting plans, co‑parenting communication, and family transitions.
    • Interpersonal Mediation: facilitation for relationship, roommate, neighbor, and community conflicts. We do not handle landlord‑tenant, short‑term stay, long‑term stay, HOA, co‑op, or condominium matters.
    • Workplace & Organizational Facilitation: communication support for teams, supervisors, and staff navigating conflict or change. We serve non‑consumer‑facing companies and organizations only.
    • Conflict Coaching: one‑on‑one support to strengthen communication, decision‑making, boundary setting, and conflict‑management skills.
