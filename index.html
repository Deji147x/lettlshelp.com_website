Not sure which practice?

Answer three questions and we will send you to the right screening. Nothing confidential is asked before eligibility is confirmed.

  1. Who is involved?

    Individuals and families, or an organization and its leaders.

  2. Is it a consumer matter?

    If it is, we cannot assist — you get free supportive referrals instead.

  3. What kind of support?

    Mediation, arbitration, facilitation, or conflict coaching.
