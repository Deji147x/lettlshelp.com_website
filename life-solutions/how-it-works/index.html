How It Works

How mediation with Transformative Life Solutions works

A clear, supportive process, from your first message to your first session.

What to expect

  1. Reach out

    Call or text (240) 650-0007, send an email, or begin intake online.

  2. Eligibility screening

    Written and verbal screening confirms your matter is one we can ethically facilitate.

  3. Confirm next steps

    If it's a fit, we plan next steps together. If not, we share free supportive referrals.

  4. Meet virtually or in person

    Choose the setting that feels right for you.

Step one

Begin intake online

Our online screening asks about the services you need, availability, safety, and eligibility. We review every response and reply with next steps or free referrals.

Start the screening

If we can't help, we'll point you to support

If we are unable to facilitate your matter, we will provide free supportive referrals. Suggested referrals may include:

  • Maryland's Consumer Protection DivisionGet help with a consumer, health billing, or insurance complaint.
  • Maryland Courts Self‑Help CenterLearn how the court system can assist with your dispute through litigation, arbitration, and mediation.
  • People's Law LibraryGet free legal help and information for Maryland cases.
  • Community Mediation MarylandObtain mediation services through centers across Maryland.
  • MPME‑rostered ADR practitionersFind a Maryland-based ADR practitioner who can assist.
  • Better Business BureauGet help resolving a problem with a business through mediation and arbitration programs.
  • Crisis Text LineReach a crisis counselor by text, 24/7.

Before you begin

  • We provide mediation services only. We do not provide legal advice, legal representation, or legal advocacy.
  • We provide coaching and training services only. We do not provide medical or mental health counseling services.
